# Stakeholders
## Volunteers - role: volunteering for events and activities - direct user: yes: needs/cares - The volunteer needs to know what event/activity the are attending to help.
## Organizers - role: helping organist events and activities - direct user: yes: needs/cares - organizers need to know who is available.
## Event Manager - role: main organizer of events - direct user: yes - needs/cares: The event manager cares about having the right volunteers with the best experience and skills.
## Activity Manager - role: main organizer of Activities - direct user: yes - needs/cares: The activities manager cares about having the right volunteers with the best experience and skills
## Director of the organization - role: The main person running the organization - direct user: no - needs/cares: the make the system fully functional and cost-effective.
## Sign up Manager - role: sorts out all sign up applications for the organization - direct user: yes - needs/cares: they need to have all sign ups in one location as to not cause confusion.

# Requirements
## FR01	The system shall allow the volunteer to create an account supplying their information
## FR02	The system shall allow a volunteer to add in their skills and experience.
## FR03	The system shall allow volunteers to change their information and contact details
## FR04	The system shall allow volunteers to show the availability times they have .
## FR05	The system shall allow organizers create new events and activities
## FR06	The system shall allow organizers to cancel or change and event or activity
## NFR01	The system shall not have all volunteer information made public and only authorized organizers can view them.
